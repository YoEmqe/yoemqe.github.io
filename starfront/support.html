Support

Contact Starfront support

For a bug, purchase issue or privacy request, email Emre Öztürk using the contact below. Include your platform, device model, game version, sector or mode, and what happened. Please omit passwords, payment details and private account credentials.

Restore Remove Ads

Use the same Apple or Google store account that made the purchase. Open Starfront → Settings → Restore while online. Removal applies to automatic between-flight ads. You can still choose to watch a video to continue a lost flight.

Continue videos

When eligible, choose Watch Ad to Continue on the results screen. A completed rewarded video resumes the flight. Each run allows up to two continues. Availability depends on the network, ad supply and the ad settings on your device.

Deep Signal leaderboard

Play Endless while signed in to Game Center on iOS or Play Games Services on Android. At the end of a flight, open the Deep Signal leaderboard from the Endless briefing to check your score.
