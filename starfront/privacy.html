Starfront Defense · Support · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Game progress

Starfront Defense stores your campaign progress, ship choices, settings, high score and cached ad-removal status on your device. Removing app data clears local progress. Device backups may retain copies according to your system settings.

Leaderboards

Deep Signal uses Apple Game Center or Google Play Games Services. When signed in, the game submits your Endless score to the platform leaderboard. The platform associates it with your player identifier and can display your gamer profile and score to other players according to your platform settings.

Advertising and choices

Ads are off by default. Players aged 18 or older can enable them in Settings. Google AdMob and its consent service may process IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, consent choices, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention. An IP address may indicate approximate location. Available choices depend on region and device settings. Turn ads off in Settings, or open Ad Privacy Options when available to revisit consent.

Purchases

Apple or Google handles billing. The game uses product, transaction and entitlement information to unlock Remove Ads and restore ownership. Payment card details stay with the payment provider. Remove Ads covers automatic between-flight ads. Rewarded continue videos are optional.

Support and retention

If you email Emre Öztürk, your address, message and any attachments are used to answer the request and troubleshoot the game. Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations. Store, advertising and gaming services handle data under their own retention policies.

Your choices

Manage your gaming profile, purchase history, advertising permissions and backups through your platform account. To request access, correction or deletion of information held by the developer, including a support message, use the contact address below and identify the request. The developer can assist with requests about data under their control.
